Frequently Asked Questions

What to Know
Before You Book

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as possible. The ideal timeline is at least one month in advance to allow proper scheduling and preparation. Shorter timelines are considered on a case by case basis.

What types of events does Gift host?

Gift works across a wide range of events including corporate conferences, community gatherings, faith-based events, panel discussions, brand activations, and speaking engagements.

Where is Gift available?

Gift is based in Atlanta, Georgia and is available for events locally and across the United States.

How long does it take to hear back?

All inquiries are personally reviewed and responded to within 48 hours. For time-sensitive requests, please make note of your event date in the form.

Does Gift do virtual events?

Yes. Gift hosts both in-person and virtual events. Please indicate the format in your inquiry.

Details

Deposit and Payment

A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your date. The balance is due 7 days before the event.

Cancellations

Cancellations made less than 14 days before the event date may be subject to the full booking fee. Please reach out as early as possible if your plans change.

Contract

A formal agreement will be provided once your inquiry is approved. No date is confirmed until the contract is signed and the deposit is received.
